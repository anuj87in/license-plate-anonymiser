License Plate Anonymiser

Upload a street or vehicle photo and every detected license plate is blurred automatically. A YOLOv8 detection model runs entirely in your browser.

How it works

  1. Detect. A YOLOv8s model finds every license plate in the image.
  2. Pad. Each box is enlarged by 15% on every side, so a slightly tight box cannot leave a plate edge readable.
  3. Blur. A Gaussian blur, as wide as the plate itself, is applied inside each box only. The rest of the image is untouched.

The confidence threshold defaults to 0.2: a missed plate is a privacy risk, while an extra blur is harmless. Detections below 0.5 are marked review (green and amber boxes are shown on screen only, never in the download).

Limitations

  • This is a prototype. Very small or distant plates (under about 40 pixels wide) are the most likely to be missed.
  • Plates that are rotated, heavily covered (for example by a garland) or on the front of some motorbikes can also be missed; the training data contained few such cases.
  • Always check the result before publishing a photo. Lower the confidence slider if a plate is not blurred.
  • The first run downloads the model; later visits load it from the browser cache.
